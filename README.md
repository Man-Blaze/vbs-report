### Vanuatu Bureau of Standards - Digital Reporting System

**Live at:** [https://man-blaze.github.io/vbs-report/](https://man-blaze.github.io/vbs-report/)

**Status:** 🟢 Production Live • Version 1.0 • Since 22 April 2026

---

## 📌 Overview

**VBS Digital Shield** is a comprehensive, sovereign digital reporting platform built for the Vanuatu Bureau of Standards. It replaces paper-based reporting with real-time digital workflows, enabling transparent, auditable, and efficient operations across all divisions.

### Why This Matters

> *"Compliance is not a penalty. It is our passport to the world."*

Vanuatu's quality infrastructure is the foundation for:
- 🇻🇺 **Kava exports** worth billions in global markets
- 🌾 **Agricultural products** meeting international standards
- 🛡️ **Consumer protection** through food safety compliance
- 📦 **Trade facilitation** through conformity assessment

VBS Digital Shield ensures every inspection, test result, and certification is tracked, verified, and audit-ready.

## 🎯 Key Features

### 🏛️ Division-Specific Modules

| Module | Purpose | Users |
|:-------|:--------|:------|
| **Provincial** | Daily inspection reports, farm visits, non-compliance tracking | Inspectors • Sanma/Malampa |
| **Laboratory** | Test results, sample tracking, equipment status, reagent levels | Santo • Port Vila Labs |
| **Packhouse** | Volume received/processed/exported, waste, temperature, staff | Santo Packhouse |
| **Standards & Certification** | Standards development, certifications issued, stakeholder engagement | S&C Division |
| **Administration** | Leave requests, health check-ins, budget, supplies, vehicles | HR • Finance • Operations |
| **Inspection** | Compliance checks, entity inspections, enforcement actions | Quality Assurance |
| **General Activity Log** | Meetings, training, stakeholder coordination | All Divisions |

### 🧪 Lab Management

| Module | Purpose |
|:-------|:--------|
| **Lab Inventory** | Track chemicals, glassware, equipment, and stock levels |
| **Reagent Usage** | Monitor consumption and trigger reorder alerts |
| **Equipment Calibration** | Track calibration schedules and maintenance |

### 👔 Management & Oversight

| Module | Purpose |
|:-------|:--------|
| **Manager Dashboard** | Review and approve/reject reports with comments |
| **CEO Dashboard** | Live dashboard - export volumes, pass rates, staff performance |
| **Activity Tracker** | Complete audit trail - who submitted, who approved, when |
| **Timesheet** | Clock In / Clock Out - Kam In / Kam Out |
