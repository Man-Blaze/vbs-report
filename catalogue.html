A Message from the CEO

The successful arrival, biosecurity clearance, and retail launch of Vanuatu's first taro shipment into Australia marks the operational beginning of a new national export pathway built under the Santo National Standard Packhouse System.

From farm traceability, standards, biosecurity, customs, and cold-chain logistics to final market presentation in Melbourne, this achievement demonstrates that Vanuatu can now deliver high-quality products through a fully coordinated international export system – opening a new era of sustainable trade, farmer income, and economic transformation for the nation.
